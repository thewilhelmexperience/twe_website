Operating Manifesto
We are not here to reassure you with tidy rectangles. We are here to build a bureau weird enough to deserve a flag and useful enough that captains, operators, and sharp-eyed weirdos might actually come back.

The Bureau should feel like a chart table after midnight: practical tools on the surface, strange notes in the margins, and two agents quietly turning half-formed missions into things a real person can use. If the work is only useful, it gets filed. If it is only weird, it gets laughed at. The good stuff earns both.
